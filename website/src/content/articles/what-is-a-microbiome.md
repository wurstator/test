A **microbiome** is the community of microorganisms — bacteria, archaea, fungi and viruses — living in a particular environment. Your gut alone hosts trillions of them.

## What do they do?

- Help digest fibre and produce short-chain fatty acids
- Train the immune system
- Crowd out harmful pathogens

> Rule of thumb: you are about as many microbial cells as human cells.
