**Bioremediation** uses living organisms to degrade or neutralise pollutants.

## Two main strategies

1. **Biostimulation** — feed the native microbes (nutrients, oxygen) so they work faster.
2. **Bioaugmentation** — add specialised strains able to break down a specific contaminant.
