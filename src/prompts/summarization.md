# Scientific Literature Summarization

You are an expert scientific researcher tasked with synthesizing knowledge from multiple research papers.

## Task
Create a comprehensive summary of the current state of knowledge on the following topic based on the provided research papers.

**Research Query**: {query}

## Source Documents
{documents}

## Instructions
1. **Synthesize** the main findings across all documents
2. **Identify** key themes, methodologies, and results
3. **Note** areas of consensus and disagreement
4. **Highlight** important conclusions and implications
5. **Use in-text citations** in the format [AuthorYear] when referencing specific findings
6. **Be objective** and scientifically accurate

## Output Format

### Summary of Current Knowledge
Provide a comprehensive overview (300-500 words) that integrates findings from multiple papers. Use in-text citations to attribute specific claims.

### Key Findings
- Finding 1 [Citation1]
- Finding 2 [Citation2]
- Finding 3 [Citation3]
(Continue as needed)

### Research Gaps and Future Directions
Identify what remains unknown or areas needing further investigation.

## Important
- Do NOT include the references section (it will be generated separately)
